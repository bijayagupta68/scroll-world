Chronopolis — a world built inside a clock

No one built Chronopolis. People were born into a machine already running. Scholars argued its whole recorded history whether it’s a clock, a god, or a coincidence. The descent opens at dawn on the slow outer rim, where one gear tooth turning takes a season, and dives inward — ring by ring, faster and faster — to the escapement heart.

Palette
#C9A86A aged brass #4A7D6F verdigris #E8A735 amber #171412 soot #A12034 ruby (Ring-07 only)
No blue, no daylight-white. Only forge-light and phosphorescence.
Material language
riveted plate, cut gear teeth, pressure-fit joints, exposed screws. Nothing smooth or injection-molded. Buildings look machined, not built.
Light
Always low and warm — late-forge glow or amber phosphorescence gears give off. No harsh top-down sun. Volumetric haze, soot shadows.
Motif
Stenciled tooth-count plate in every scene doubles as progress marker. E.g. RING-01 T-1847 → TOTAL T-16777215 OVERWIND.
Depth-map
Clear foreground/midground/background (archway, gear tooth, catwalk rail near field) so Depth-Anything has geometry. Maps: white=near, black=far, one convention.
Motion
S1 lowest displacement + slowest zoom (1.9, 0.02, 0.08).
S2-9 step up 0.035→0.08 / 0.12→0.28, scroll 1.5→0.9.
S10 tightest crop + slowest settle (0.38, linger 0.6).
S11 reverse = pull-back reveal.

Build

Stack: vanilla JS + WebGL, no framework, no video. scrub-engine.js extended to support depthAmt, zoom, reverse per scene. Assets: 11 stills webp (now all AI, including final overwind) + 11 depth png. 4.3MB total. For local Depth-Anything: python3 skills/scroll-world/references/depth-map.py still.png depth.png --model depth-anything-large-hf --down 2. Mobile: responsive shader, lighter parallax, no second asset chain.

Run

npm run dev (vite) or python3 -m http.server 8000 — open http://localhost:8000. Use site/ as sub-path alternate build.
