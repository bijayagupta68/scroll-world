Material: riveted plate, cut gear teeth, pressure-fit joints, exposed screws. Light: low warm forge glow / amber phosphorescence. Motif: stenciled tooth-count plate in every scene doubles as progress marker.

Motion mapping: Scene 1 lowest depth-displacement + slowest zoom (scroll 1.9, depth 0.02, zoom 0.08). Scenes 2–9 step up: 0.035→0.08 depth, 0.12→0.28 zoom, scroll shortens 1.5→0.9. Scene 10 tightest crop (0.38) + slowest settle linger 0.6. Scene 11 reverse true = pull-back reveal instead of push-in.

Assets: 11 stills (10 AI via generate_image + 1 composite overwind) + 11 synthetic depth maps (white=near, black=far, PIL-generated with per-scene foreground masks to give Depth-Anything real geometry). No video, no credits. Shader synthesizes camera at runtime.
